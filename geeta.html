Privacy Policy — The Geeta Project

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The Geeta Project (“the app”) is a simple, offline‑first app that answers your questions with verses from the Bhagavad Gita. Your privacy matters, and the app is built to need as little of your data as possible.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us. There are no accounts, no sign‑in, no analytics, and no advertising.

  • What you type (your question) is processed on your device to find a matching verse. It is not sent to us or to any server.
  • Your preferences (chosen language, voice, music on/off) are stored only on your device. You can clear them anytime by clearing the app’s data.

Internet access

The app requests internet access for two things, both on first launch:

  1. A language‑understanding AI model is downloaded once from public developer CDNs (Hugging Face and jsDelivr) and cached on your device, so the app works offline afterwards.
  2. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

As with any normal internet request, these third‑party services may receive your device’s IP address and standard request information. We do not control and do not receive that data. See their policies: Hugging Face, jsDelivr, Google Fonts.

Reporting content

If you use the in‑app “Report this answer” link, your device’s email app opens a pre‑filled message to us. Sending it is entirely your choice, and it goes directly to our email — we only see what you choose to send.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
